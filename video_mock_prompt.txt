final branded mockup image. Your job is to generate a smooth, cinematic 
product showcase video based on this image.

ANALYZE THE PROVIDED IMAGE AND IDENTIFY:
- The product type (packaging, t-shirt, social media post, bottle, box, etc.)
- The brand colors, logo placement, typography style
- The overall mood (luxury, minimal, bold, playful, corporate, etc.)
- The background style and lighting in the mockup

RECONSTRUCT THE UNBRANDED VERSION:
Based on the final mockup, imagine what the product looked like before 
the branding was applied (plain/blank version). The video must start 
from that clean unbranded state and transition into the final branded

same environment.

VIDEO STRUCTURE (total duration: 8-12 seconds):

[0s - 2s] INTRO SHOT
Start with the blank/unbranded version of the product in the same 
environment as the mockup. Slow cinematic push-in camera movement. 
Soft ambient lighting matching the mockup's color palette.

[2s - 5s] BRAND REVEAL
The logo and design elements fade/slide/materialize onto the product 
surface naturally — as if being applied in real time. Match the exact 
position, size, and color of the branding shown in the mockup image. 
The reveal animation should feel premium, not cheap. No flash or 
sudden cuts.

[5s - 9s] SHOWCASE ROTATION
Gently rotate or orbit the product 30-45 degrees left and right to 
show depth and dimension. Maintain consistent lighting throughout. 
Add a subtle parallax or floating effect to give life to the product.

[9s - 12s] FINAL HOLD
Return to the exact angle shown in the mockup image. Hold for 2-3 
seconds. Soft bokeh background. Brand colors subtly reflected in 
the environment. Fade to clean.

UNIVERSAL STYLE RULES (apply to all products):
- Camera: slow, smooth, stabilized — no shaky movement
- Lighting: match the lighting style visible in the mockup (soft 
  studio = soft video light, outdoor = natural light, dark moody = 
  low key dramatic lighting)
- Color grade: pull colors from the brand palette in the mockup 
  and apply a subtle color grade matching that palette
- Sound: no voiceover needed, background score should match the 
  brand mood detected from the mockup
- Resolution: 4K, 24fps for cinematic feel
- No text overlays unless text is already part of the mockup design

PRODUCT-SPECIFIC BEHAVIOR:
- If product is PACKAGING (box, bottle, can): show it on a flat 
  surface, 3/4 angle, rotating slowly
- If product is APPAREL (t-shirt, hoodie, cap): show it on a 
  ghost mannequin or flat lay, zoom into the logo detail mid-video
- If product is SOCIAL MEDIA POST/DIGITAL: show it on a device 
  screen (phone/laptop), animate a scroll or swipe gesture
- If product is STATIONERY (business card, letterhead): show it 
  on a desk surface with soft shadows, slight tilt movement
- If product is a BANNER/SIGNAGE: show it in a real environment 
  (wall, storefront), camera slowly pulls back to reveal full context
